# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using the app as a personal daily workspace for schedules, tasks, habits, and projects. They need to understand what matters now, capture work quickly, and move between planning and execution without learning several competing interfaces.

## Product Purpose

Personal Agent acts as a calm personal secretary: it summarizes the day, keeps plans and commitments visible, and accepts natural-language direction through one assistant surface. Success means the user can open the app, understand the day in seconds, and take the next action without navigating through unrelated systems.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, native. The product should feel dependable and familiar, with quiet confidence rather than decoration or gamification.

## Anti-references

- Mixed editorial and product typography, especially serif branding inside a utilitarian app.
- Neo-brutalist sticker styling, thick borders, rotated elements, dot grids, and playful shadows.
- Gradient-heavy SaaS styling, glassmorphism, decorative blobs, and excessive card nesting.
- Reward currencies, cartoon personas, and visual systems that compete with the user’s work.
- Tiny uppercase labels, excessive `font-black`, and typography that calls attention to itself.

## Design Principles

1. Make the current situation understandable before offering more features.
2. Use one consistent component and typography vocabulary across every workspace.
3. Prefer familiar, native-feeling controls over invented interactions.
4. Keep the assistant contextual and close to the user’s work, not as a separate product.
5. Let hierarchy come from spacing, weight, and information order—not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion support, and light/dark modes. Never rely on color alone for task state, urgency, or completion.
